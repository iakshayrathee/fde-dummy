## The argument

**1. The reframe.** He already has a manager: the dispatch algorithm. But look at *which half* of management got
automated. A manager allocates, measures and punishes; a manager also coaches, explains, advocates and protects. The
gig economy automated the first half brilliantly and deleted the second.

> **It kept the whip and threw away the hand.**

So this isn't adding AI to work — it's **restoring the half that was thrown away**, which is only possible now because
the deleted half always needed language, memory and judgement.

**2. But does he even want it?** "No boss" is why many riders chose this work. A boss has authority *over* you; this
has authority *for* you — and the test is **can he tell it to shut up?** If he can silence it and it still files his
claim, it isn't supervision, it's **staff.** He's not getting a manager, he's getting a manager *of his own*.
Default is quiet: money and exceptions only, everything else opt-in.

**3. Three things convert software into a manager.**
- **Memory** — two graphs, him and the city. The city graph compounds across all 50,000 and is the moat. It's also the
  poisoning surface, so facts corroborate before they propagate.
- **Authority** — commercially, *what's the spending limit?* Technically, **the agent has no write access.** Waiving a
  penalty is a write to payments; renegotiating a delivery is a write to the OMS. Authority inside an enterprise is an
  **API scope plus a spend limit** — which makes this an integration project wearing a voice interface.
- **Accountability** — the one everyone skips. You can't make an agent loyal with a system prompt. Put loyalty in the
  **scorecard**: earnings/hour, 90-day retention, progression, overrule rate. Never throughput. Each paired with a
  guardrail, because Goodhart applies to my metrics too. **The agent's boss is the driver.**

**4. Seven things that don't exist today.** No login ever · the shift is dead (he declares an outcome, the agent solves
backwards) · agents negotiate with each other · the commute is the classroom · **the collective** (400 riders'
invisible complaints become one evidenced case — operational friction only, never rates, and that limit is what
protects it) · trust by verified prediction, not explanation · his skill record is portable.

**5. The hard edge.** Platforms already punish — *silently*, via deprioritisation, nothing to appeal. The real humane
upgrade is punishment that's spoken, specific and appealable. An agent that only advocates is a lawyer, not a manager.

**6. The hard part isn't the AI.** State reconciliation (if the OMS write fails, he doesn't get paid), idempotency over
a lossy channel, offline authority, memory poisoning, latency tiering — plus *how do you regression-test a manager?*
And the regulatory surface, because documenting working conditions creates a discoverable evidence trail.

**7. Commercial.** ₹30 Cr attrition exposure → ₹12 Cr defensible → ₹2–4 Cr contract, **priced on retention so the
contract mirrors the scorecard.** And they can't build it in-house: they can build the voice, but **neutrality can't be
built by the people who sign the paycheque.**

**8. Ship.** Ride pillion, read 500 tickets, then one wedge to 200 drivers. *You already have the mouth — this needs
hands and a memory.*

## Before you record

1. **Interview three riders.** [`2-produce/rider-interview-kit.md`](2-produce/rider-interview-kit.md) — where to go,
   what to ask, pay them ₹200–300. There's a 12-second slot at `[0:48]`. This is the largest single gap in the
   submission. **If they contradict the design, say so on camera and show the change** — that's the most hireable ten
   seconds available to you.
2. **Build the cold open and close in [Hunar's voice playground](https://playground.hunar.ai/).** Hindi or Marathi,
   English subtitles throughout.
3. **Rehearse against `likely-questions.md`,** and be able to rebuild any number in the pack from memory. A polished
   submission raises a bar you then have to clear live.
4. **Time it aloud, twice. Under 5:00.** Overrunning a stated limit is itself a signal.
