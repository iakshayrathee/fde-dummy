## Delivery notes

- **Two on-camera moments only**, at 0:20 and 4:11. Same framing both times.
- **The cold open must not be explained.** No "let me set the scene." Twenty
  seconds of a call, then cut. The half-second pause before *"अच्छा… ठीक है"* is the
  most persuasive moment in the video.
- **Eleven bolded lines are the landing points.** Slow down, drop pitch, small
  pause after each. They are the only sentences that must survive.
- **Numbers spoken in full and shown on card simultaneously.**
- **Sources on screen** wherever a statistic appears. Small, bottom-left.
- **Time yourself per block, not end to end.** Section-level drift is what pushed
  v1 thirty-five seconds over without anyone noticing.
