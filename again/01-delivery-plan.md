## Format concept

**"Hear the product, don't see it."**

If the argument is that these workers cannot read, then the video should be
something you can largely *hear*. Structure follows from that:

- **Cold open** ~25s of simulated voice call — a worker asks why her pay is lower
  this month; the AI answers in Hindi. Black screen, waveform, English subtitles.
  No intro, no title card. The argument lands before a word is said about you.
- **Each sub-question** gets one short call moment + one card carrying three
  things: mechanism, failure mode, metric.
- **Middle section** is voiceover over cards and call audio.
- **Close** on camera: what ships first, what gets measured.

Production: phone camera for bookends, any recorder for voice, CapCut or DaVinci
Resolve for edit, Canva or Google Slides for cards. Export MP4, 16:9, 1080p.

Companion artifact: one-page PDF sent with the video link. Low cost, signals the
polish an FDE brings to a customer.
