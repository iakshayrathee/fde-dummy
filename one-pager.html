Challenge #3 — Audio-First Workforce System for Non-Literate Factory Workers
Companion to the 5-minute video.

The Reality

  • Customer: multi-plant apparel manufacturer, 100,000 workers, mostly migrant women. Wages = piece rate + attendance incentive − statutory deductions.
  • Two populations, not one. 40% can't read English → a translation problem, solved with vernacular. 20% (20,000 people) can't read any language → the real design problem; translation does nothing for them.
  • Literacy-assuming SaaS produces wage confusion, attrition, and production stoppages.
Reading is the accommodation. Listening is the baseline.
Audio over shared or personal phones + passive sensing at the plant. Zero new hardware on the floor.
  • The interaction rule: She never composes. She only confirms. Odia ASR sits near 35% word error (vs Hindi ~16%), so open speech never touches anything consequential. For consent she must produce a number — a number can't be faked by nodding.

The Five Workflows

  • Onboarding — the contract. Forms + thumbprints are coerced. Replace with a recorded audio disclosure she can replay; her voice on it becomes her key. Comprehension tested by arithmetic ("take two days off — how much less?"), not "do you understand?"
  • Attendance — no unmeasured biometrics. Work destroys fingerprints (~36% verification failure, Telangana). Presence is passive: she walks in, she's present. Every supervisor override triggers an evening verification call to her — the audit loop that makes discretion visible; three gate failures auto-schedule re-enrolment. Absence triggers a cost-preview: "an unpaid day lowers your bonus by ~₹250" — while she can still choose. Measure false-non-match at their gate for two weeks, by cohort. Never the average.
  • Salary — explain the delta. Not a payslip. Proactively explain why this month differs from last (absence, PF, overtime), in rupees and days. "PF is your money, it stays in your name" — one sentence between savings and theft. Verified First: nothing about pay is spoken until voiceprint (phone) or the gate (line-side screen) confirms it's her — so a shared handset never hears her pay.
  • Training — demonstration, not instruction. Non-literate ≠ non-skilled. Silent video at the machine + narration as a separate audio track: shoot once, localise into 8 languages for 8 recordings. Metric = defect rate over 50 pieces. Never measure completion.
  • Trust — track record over tone. Her distrust is rational; she's been lied to by a recruiting agent. The AI says it's a machine, borrows authority from her supervisor, ends every call with one key to a named human, and never delivers termination or discipline (unions here have documented forced resignations — a commitment they can enforce). Monthly it advocates: "₹340 of last month's overtime never reached you — I've sent it to payroll." An AI that only enforces is a supervisor. An AI that advocates is a manager.

Business Logic & Metrics

  • Sequencing: salary variance calls first — one plant, no hardware, uses payroll data they already have. Attendance capex goes after trust exists. Make it useful to her before it's useful to them.
  • Unit economics: ~₹350 per worker per year — under one day of her wages.
  • Breakeven: must prevent ~4 attrition points/year to pay for itself — falsifiable against their own numbers.
  • The blindspot: ~38% of workers at large Indian factories are contract labour. If a contractor holds their pay data, the salary wedge can't reach them — the most precarious people in the building. Week-one discovery measures this share first; if high, attendance ships before salary.
  • North-star KPI: Can she state her own pay, unprompted, before payday? Everything else is a proxy.
